About Our Group

All Nippon Virtual Group (ANVG)

All Nippon Virtual Group is an independent, community-driven virtual aviation organization operating on the Waypoints platform. Inspired by the rich legacy of Japanese aviation, ANVG unites multiple distinctive airline operations into a single cohesive group structure.

Whether piloting domestic island feeders, crossing oceans in wide-body flagships, flying high-density low-cost leisure corridors, or commanding transpacific cargo freighters, ANVG offers a versatile and welcoming environment where pilots of all experience levels can thrive.

Our Group Pillars

Four distinctive operating divisions under All Nippon Virtual Group

Flagship Mainline

All Nippon Airways

The core foundation of ANVG. Providing full-service passenger operations with an expansive domestic trunk network and premium long-haul routes connecting Tokyo Haneda (RJTT) and Narita (RJAA) across Asia, North America, and Europe.

Hubs: RJTT, RJAA Fleet: B77W, B787, A320, Q400
Heavy Freight Division

Nippon Cargo Airlines (NCA)

Dedicated heavy-lift freight logistics connecting Japan with global supply chains. Operating the Boeing 747-8F across high-payload transpacific corridors connecting Tokyo Narita with Anchorage (PANC) and Chicago O'Hare (KORD).

Hubs: RJAA, PANC Fleet: Boeing 747-8F
Low-Cost & Regional

Peach Aviation

ANVG's vibrant low-cost subsidiary providing affordable, high-frequency domestic flights and regional hops across East Asia. Headquartered out of Osaka Kansai (RJBB) and Naha (ROAH) with signature point-to-point agility.

Hubs: RJBB, ROAH, RJAA Fleet: Airbus A320-200
Leisure Medium-Haul

AirJapan

The hybrid leisure airline of the group, bridging full-service comfort with budget accessibility. Operating medium-haul Boeing 787 flights linking Tokyo Narita to vacation hotspots throughout Southeast Asia and Oceania.

Hubs: RJAA (Narita) Fleet: Boeing 787-8
