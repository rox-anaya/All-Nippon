About Us

All Nippon Virtual Group (ANVG)

All Nippon Virtual Group is a Japanese-inspired virtual aviation group built for the Infinite Flight community. ANVG brings multiple virtual airline operations together under one group, with a focus on realistic flight operations, Japanese aviation culture, professionalism, and an enjoyable pilot experience.

From domestic regional flights to long-haul international and cargo operations, our goal is to create a connected virtual airline network where every pilot has a place to fly, progress, and be part of something bigger.
