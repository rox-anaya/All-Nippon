The Wings of ANVG

Spanning full-service passenger travel, dedicated transpacific freight, and regional low-cost routes. Discover the aircraft you will command as an ANVG pilot.

All Nippon Airways — Mainline

Flagship full-service domestic & international operations

Dash 8-Q400

ANA Regional

Operated primarily under ANVG's regional wings, the Dash 8-Q400 is the ultimate regional workhorse. It executes short hops with incredible efficiency, connecting smaller islands and regional airports where larger jets cannot operate.

A320-200

Short-Haul

A versatile and highly reliable narrow-body, the A320-200 provides a perfect balance of capacity and frequency. It handles a mix of high-demand domestic sectors and key short-haul international routes across our network.

B737-800

Domestic Core

The backbone of ANVG's domestic network. The Boeing 737-800 flies high-frequency routes across the virtual archipelago, offering proven reliability and quick turnarounds to keep the domestic timetable running smoothly.

B787-8 Dreamliner

Mid/Long-Haul

A pioneer in modern aviation, the B787-8 is highly adaptable. It serves both high-density domestic routes and pioneers thin, long-haul international connections across the expanding ANVG virtual network.

B787-9 Dreamliner

Long-Haul

The stretched variant of the Dreamliner family. Offering greater range and increased passenger capacity, the B787-9 forms the modern core of ANVG's international long-haul network, connecting our hubs to Europe and the Americas.

B787-10 Dreamliner

High-Density

The longest of the Dreamliner family, maximizing passenger capacity while maintaining exceptional fuel efficiency. It is optimized for high-demand routes to major destinations across Asia and heavily trafficked domestic trunk routes.

B777-200ER

Heavy Domestic

A massive twin-engine heavy traditionally utilized by ANVG for moving large numbers of passengers on high-demand domestic trunk routes, as well as serving select mid-range international sectors.

B777-300ER

Flagship

The true flagship of ANVG's international long-haul fleet. The 777-300ER operates the most prestigious and longest routes in the network, routinely flying transpacific and transatlantic journeys to North America and Europe.

Nippon Cargo Airlines (NCA)

Dedicated heavy freight division across Pacific trade routes

Official Livery in IF

Boeing 747-8F

Heavy Freighter

The legendary Queen of the Skies commanding ANVG's heavy cargo logistics. Connecting Tokyo Narita (RJAA) to Anchorage (PANC) and Chicago (KORD) with massive nose-loading and main-deck freight capacity.

Peach Aviation

High-frequency low-cost & leisure hops across Japan and East Asia

Official Livery in IF

Airbus A320-200

LCC Regional

Sporting signature fuchsia wings out of Kansai (RJBB) and Naha (ROAH), Peach connects travelers to scenic domestic getaways and East Asian destinations with unmatched point-to-point agility.

AirJapan

Medium-haul leisure connections to Southeast Asia and Oceania

Standard B787 Airframe

Boeing 787-8

Leisure Long-Haul

Combining full Dreamliner comfort with low-cost efficiency, AirJapan bridges Tokyo Narita with Bangkok (VTBS), Singapore (WSSS), and popular Asian leisure corridors.
