IFC Profile

The Wings of ANVG

From nimble regional turboprops navigating island approaches to massive wide-body flagships crossing oceans, our fleet is diverse, modern, and equipped for any mission. Discover the aircraft you will command as an ANVG pilot.

Dash 8-Q400

Regional

Operated primarily under ANA Wings, the Dash 8-Q400 is the ultimate regional workhorse. It executes short hops with incredible efficiency, connecting Japan's smaller islands and regional airports where larger jets cannot operate.

A320-200

Short-Haul

A versatile and highly reliable narrow-body, the A320-200 provides a perfect balance of capacity and frequency. It handles a mix of high-demand domestic sectors and key short-haul international routes across Asia.

B737-800

Short-Haul

The backbone of ANA's domestic network. The Boeing 737-800 flies high-frequency routes across the Japanese archipelago, offering proven reliability and quick turnarounds to keep the domestic timetable running smoothly.

B787-8 Dreamliner

Mid/Long-Haul

As the global launch customer, ANA revolutionized air travel with the B787-8. This aircraft is highly adaptable, serving both high-density domestic routes within Japan and pioneering thin, long-haul international routes.

B787-9 Dreamliner

Long-Haul

The stretched variant of the Dreamliner family. Offering greater range and increased passenger capacity, the B787-9 forms the modern core of ANA's international long-haul network, connecting Japan to Europe and the Americas.

B787-10 Dreamliner

High-Density

The longest of the Dreamliner family, maximizing passenger capacity while maintaining exceptional fuel efficiency. It is optimized for high-demand routes to major destinations across Asia and heavily trafficked domestic trunk routes.

B777-200ER

Heavy

A massive twin-engine heavy traditionally utilized by ANA for moving large numbers of passengers on high-demand domestic trunk routes, as well as serving select mid-range international sectors.

B777-300ER

Flagship

The true flagship of ANA's international long-haul fleet. The 777-300ER operates the most prestigious and longest routes in the network, routinely flying transpacific and transatlantic journeys to North America and Europe.

B777F (Cargo)

Logistics

The powerhouse of ANA Cargo. This dedicated heavy freighter drives our global logistics network, capable of carrying massive payloads across continents to keep the world's supply chains moving.
