Join Our Flight Deck

Ready to Fly? Think you have what it takes? Check the pilot requirements below, review our onboarding process, and submit your application to become part of the All Nippon Virtual Group family.

Pilot Requirements

01

Minimum Age: Must be 13 years old or older, in accordance with official Infinite Flight Community rules.

02

Trust Level: Must be an active member in good standing of the Infinite Flight Community (IFC).

03

Flight Grade: Must hold Grade 2 or above within Infinite Flight multiplayer.

04

Subscription: Must own a legal and active copy of Infinite Flight Pro.

05

Activity: Minimum requirement of logging at least 1 flight per month to maintain active pilot status.

06

Compliance: Must NOT be listed on the official IFVARB Blacklist or Watchlist.

What Happens Next?

1

Your application is reviewed by our staff team via the Infinite Flight Community.

2

If accepted, you will receive your initial Cadet trainee rank.

3

Complete Stage 1 theoretical exam covering basic operations and phraseology.

4

Complete Stage 2 check-ride flight evaluation (if required by the training team).

5

Receive full certification, callsign assignment, and operational rank epaulettes!

Important Pilot Note

Upon successful acceptance and completion of your initial intake, you will receive full access to our private Discord server channels, official pilot handbook, and flight logging portal. Make sure your IFC username and profile details are accurate in your application!
