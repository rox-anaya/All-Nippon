IFC Profile

Pilot Training & Evaluation

Excellence in aviation requires dedication and skill. Our structured training pipeline ensures every ANVG pilot meets the highest standards of realism, safety, and virtual airmanship.

Evaluation Stages

Stage 1

Theory Exam

A comprehensive assessment covering basic aviation knowledge, Infinite Flight procedures, radio telephony phraseology, and ANVG operational guidelines. This ensures you understand our formatting and flight logging standards before taking to the virtual skies.

* Required for all new recruits
Stage 2

Checkride Evaluation

A practical flight evaluation with a member of our training team. Depending on your experience and prior virtual airline history, a checkride may sometimes be required to demonstrate aircraft handling, traffic pattern execution, and smooth ATC communication.

* Evaluated on a case-by-case basis
