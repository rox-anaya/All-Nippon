Operational Hubs

RJTT

Tokyo International Airport (Haneda)

The premier domestic and international gateway for ANVG. Situated in the heart of Tokyo, Haneda offers highly scenic approaches over Tokyo Bay and serves as our most heavily trafficked operational base.

RJAA

Narita International Airport

Our primary heavy international hub. Narita connects Japan to the virtual world, handling the vast majority of our long-haul wide-body passenger flights and global B777F cargo logistics.

RJOO

Osaka International Airport (Itami)

Serving the Kansai region, Itami is a vital domestic hub dedicated to high-frequency regional operations. It is the perfect base for our narrow-body and turboprop pilots looking for quick, immersive short-hauls.

RJBB

Kansai International Airport

The 24-hour offshore gateway to Western Japan. Kansai supports a healthy, robust mix of domestic connections, international passenger routes, and critical global cargo operations.
